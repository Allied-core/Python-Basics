Exercises
1. Numbers 1–10
Print numbers from 1 to 10 using a loop.
Practice: for, range().
2. Multiplication Table
Take a number and print its multiplication table from 1 to 10.
Example:
5 × 1 = 5
5 × 2 = 10
...
Practice: loops + arithmetic.
3. Sum of N Numbers
Take N and calculate:
1 + 2 + 3 + ... + N
Hint: Start with total = 0.
4. Count Digits
Take an integer and count how many digits it contains.
Example:
Input: 58392
Output: 5
Hint: Think about repeatedly removing the last digit.
5. Reverse a Number
Reverse an integer.
Example:
Input: 12345
Output: 54321
Hint: % 10 gives the last digit. // 10 removes it.
