Exercises
1. Personal Introduction
Take the user's name, age and city and print a properly formatted introduction.
Practice: input, variables, strings, output.
2. Simple Calculator
Take two numbers and display:
‌Addition
‌Subtraction
‌Multiplication
‌Division
‌Remainder
Hint: The % operator gives the remainder.
3. Even or Odd
Take an integer and determine whether it is even or odd.
Hint: Think about the remainder when dividing by 2.
4. Number Classifier
Take a number and determine whether it is:
‌Positive
‌Negative
‌Zero
Hint: This is a direct if / elif / else problem.
5. Largest of Three
Take three numbers and print the largest.
Restriction: Don't use max().
Hint: Compare the numbers using conditions.
